Summary: Nano Swarm Live Wallpaper does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The simulation runs 100% locally on your device.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We respect your privacy. Nano Swarm Live Wallpaper operates entirely offline and does not collect any personally identifiable information, usage analytics, device identifiers, location data, or crash logs.

  • No user accounts or registration are required.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics) are included.
  • No advertising networks or tracking cookies are used.

2. Local Storage

Your visual and simulation preferences (such as color presets, sensor distance, or decay settings) are saved strictly locally on your device using standard Android storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your phone or tablet and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

3. Google Play Store

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect basic transaction or diagnostic information according to the Google Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your payment details.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our application collects no personal information from anyone, it is safe for users of all ages and complies with children's privacy regulations.
